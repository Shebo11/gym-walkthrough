About this model

Built directly from the design model (FBX): the building shell, wellness rooms, furniture and equipment are the real 3D model, aligned to the AutoCAD plan (13.0 m x 24.0 m footprint). Materials, colours and lighting follow the design renders.

From the model
  • Gym room 13.0 m x 19.5 m with the rounded north-east corner and full-height garden glazing.
  • Wellness block 13.0 m wide: study room, WC/shower cells, dressing with lockers, sauna and jacuzzi inside the curved outer wall.
  • Real furniture: treadmills, pilates mats, training wall with racks and stall bars, aerial hammock, and the deep-green lounge sofa in the rounded corner.
Notes
  • Floors, garden setting and planting are simplified for the web. Ceiling height follows the model (about 3.4 m).
  • Some pieces in the file were parked below the floor by the designer (e.g. the glass entry leaves); they are left out.

Controls. Tour: drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom. Walk and Fly: WASD or arrows to move, drag to look, Shift to go faster, Q/E for up and down in Fly. On a phone use the left thumb to move and the right thumb to look.
